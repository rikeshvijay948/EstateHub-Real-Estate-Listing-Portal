Frequently Asked Questions

Learn more about how EstateHub verifies properties, connects clients, and ensures transparent transactions.

How does EstateHub verify property listings?

Our field operations team conducts physical property inspections, checks DTCP/RERA approvals, validates clear legal titles, and ensures photos match the actual physical property.

Are there any hidden fees or extra brokerage markups?

No. All prices displayed on EstateHub are direct owner and verified agent asking rates with complete transparency. We do not charge hidden listing markups.

How can I schedule a private site visit?

Simply click on any property details page and choose "Contact Agent" or click "Call Directly". You can propose your convenient weekend or weekday time slot.

Can I list my own property as an owner or agent?

Yes! Click on "Sell" or "Post Property" in the top navigation bar. Fill out the property specs and photos, and it will be instantly published to your dashboard and the public search directory.
